Privacy Policy

How Lumina Dev Apps collects, uses, and protects your information across our iOS and macOS applications.

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Direct Parcel Distributors Inc. (Ontario corporation #1000218439), operating under the registered business name Lumina Dev Apps, collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website (luminadevapps.com) and our published applications on the Apple App Store and Mac App Store.

Our apps differ widely in what they do, and therefore in what data they touch. Some make no network requests at all and collect nothing; others include AI or messaging features that necessarily send data to a service provider. Each section below states which apps it applies to. Where a section does not name an app, that section does not apply to it.

By using any of our apps or this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who We Are

Lumina Dev Apps is an iOS and macOS app development studio based in Oshawa, Ontario, Canada. All apps published under the Apple Developer team ID PLV8ZGP769 with bundle ID prefix com.luminadevapps are owned and operated by Direct Parcel Distributors Inc.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

When you contact us by email, we receive the contents of your message along with your email address. Some of our apps allow you to enter data (such as inventory counts, invoices, study notes, or resume content) — this data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us unless this policy explicitly says otherwise for that app. Apps listed in the “Apps That Collect No Data” section below transmit nothing at all.

2.2 Information collected automatically

Apple may share aggregated, anonymized App Store analytics with us (downloads, crashes, device types). We do not embed third-party analytics SDKs that profile individual users.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our apps and website.
  • To respond to your support requests sent to support@luminadevapps.com.
  • To diagnose crashes and errors using anonymized data provided by Apple.
  • To comply with legal obligations under Canadian and international law.

4. AI-Powered Features (Anthropic Claude)

Important — please read carefully if you use any of our AI-powered apps.

Several of our apps include features powered by Anthropic's Claude API. This section applies only to the apps listed at the end of this section. Apps not listed there contain no AI features and send no data to any AI provider. When you use an AI feature in an app that has one:

  • The text, prompts, or content you submit to the AI feature is sent to Anthropic's servers (api.anthropic.com) to generate a response.
  • Anthropic's data handling is governed by their own Privacy Policy and Acceptable Use Policy.
  • We do not store, log, or retain your prompts or AI responses on our servers.
  • Do not submit personally identifiable patient data, financial account numbers, government IDs, or other regulated personal information to AI features.

5. Apps That Collect No Data

The apps below make no network requests of any kind. They contain no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising identifiers, and they transmit no data to us or to any third party. For these apps, the AI, third-party services and data-storage sections of this policy do not apply.
  • CardVault — Keep your cards where only you can reach them. Scan a card with the camera and CardVault reads it on-device, then locks it behind Face ID. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked, and the app makes no network requests at all — your cards never leave your iPhone. Optional encrypted backup lets you move your vault between devices with a passphrase only you know.

Data you enter in these apps is held only on your device, protected by iOS file protection and the iOS Keychain. Where an app stores particularly sensitive values, it uses the Keychain setting that keeps them out of iCloud and iTunes backups, so they cannot be restored onto another device. On-device scanning and text recognition, where offered, use Apple's Vision framework and send no image or recognised text to any server.

Where one of these apps offers an optional encrypted backup, the file is encrypted with a passphrase you choose before it leaves the app, and you choose where it is saved. That is the only circumstance in which data can leave your device, it happens only when you initiate it, and we never receive the file and cannot decrypt it. If you lose the passphrase, the file cannot be recovered.

Deleting an item removes it from your device. Deleting the app removes all of its data. Backup files you saved elsewhere are unaffected and must be deleted by you.

6. Medical & Clinical Apps Disclaimer

Our medical apps (NurseToolkit, DoctorToolkit, PharmDeck, HealthDeck) are educational and reference tools. They are not medical devices, do not diagnose or treat conditions, and must not replace clinical judgment, institutional protocols, or professional medical advice. Always verify dosages, drug interactions, and clinical decisions against authoritative sources and your local guidelines.

7. Third-Party Services

Our apps and website may interact with the following third parties:

  • Apple Inc. — for App Store distribution, in-app purchases, push notifications, and platform analytics.
  • Anthropic, PBC — for AI inference in apps that include Claude-powered features.
  • Google Fonts — our website serves the Sora and Source Code Pro typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

8. Data Storage & Security

Most app data is stored locally on your device. In the apps that do send data off the device — AI prompts to Anthropic, or messages in our messaging apps — it is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS-encrypted connections. We implement reasonable safeguards but no transmission over the internet is 100% secure. Apps listed under “Apps That Collect No Data” send nothing off the device at all.

Apps that hold sensitive information store it using the protections iOS provides for that purpose: local databases protected with FileProtectionType.complete, which are unreadable while the device is locked, and the iOS Keychain with kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly for individual secret values. Where an app copies a sensitive value to the clipboard, it is marked local to your device so it is not shared with your other Apple devices, and is cleared automatically shortly afterwards.

9. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. KidLearn and similar education apps are designed for use under parental supervision and do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Your Rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and applicable international laws (such as the EU GDPR), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your local data-protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email admin@luminadevapps.com.
