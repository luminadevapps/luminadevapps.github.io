Privacy Policy

How Lumina Dev Apps collects, uses, and protects your information across our iOS and macOS applications.

Last updated: June 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lumina Dev Apps, a brand of Direct Parcel Distributors Inc. (Ontario corporation #1000218439), collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website (luminadevapps.com) and our published applications on the Apple App Store and Mac App Store.

By using any of our apps or this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who We Are

Lumina Dev Apps is an iOS and macOS app development studio based in Oshawa, Ontario, Canada. All apps published under the Apple Developer team ID PLV8ZGP769 with bundle ID prefix com.luminadevapps are owned and operated by Direct Parcel Distributors Inc.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

When you contact us by email, we receive the contents of your message along with your email address. Some of our apps allow you to enter data (such as inventory counts, invoices, study notes, or resume content) — this data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData or equivalent local storage and is not transmitted to us unless explicitly indicated.

2.2 Information collected automatically

Apple may share aggregated, anonymized App Store analytics with us (downloads, crashes, device types). We do not embed third-party analytics SDKs that profile individual users.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our apps and website.
  • To respond to your support requests sent to support@luminadevapps.com.
  • To diagnose crashes and errors using anonymized data provided by Apple.
  • To comply with legal obligations under Canadian and international law.

4. AI-Powered Features (Anthropic Claude)

Important — please read carefully if you use any of our AI-powered apps.

Several of our apps include features powered by Anthropic's Claude API. When you use these features:

  • The text, prompts, or content you submit to the AI feature is sent to Anthropic's servers (api.anthropic.com) to generate a response.
  • Anthropic's data handling is governed by their own Privacy Policy and Acceptable Use Policy.
  • We do not store, log, or retain your prompts or AI responses on our servers.
  • Do not submit personally identifiable patient data, financial account numbers, government IDs, or other regulated personal information to AI features.

  • NurseToolkit — Clinical toolkit for nurses with medical calculators, drug references, care planning tools, and Anthropic Claude-powered clinical decision support. Backed by 30+ peer-reviewed medical citations for evidence-based practice.
  • DoctorToolkit — AI-powered clinical tools for doctors. SOAP note builder, drug interaction checker, medical calculators, and Anthropic Claude-powered differential diagnosis assistant — all in one app.
  • MedStudy AI — AI-powered medical study companion. SM-2 spaced repetition with 23 seeded flashcard decks across 8 medical subjects, AI-generated cards, and an OSCE simulator. BYOK Claude API.
  • MathSolverAI — AI-powered math tutor. Snap a photo of a problem and get clear step-by-step explanations powered by Anthropic Claude. Algebra, calculus, geometry, statistics, and more.
  • AI Resume Builder — Build a stunning resume with Claude AI. Multiple professional PDF templates, live preview, and one-tap AI enhancement to rewrite your resume content for maximum impact.

5. Medical & Clinical Apps Disclaimer

Our medical apps (NurseToolkit, DoctorToolkit, PharmDeck, HealthDeck) are educational and reference tools. They are not medical devices, do not diagnose or treat conditions, and must not replace clinical judgment, institutional protocols, or professional medical advice. Always verify dosages, drug interactions, and clinical decisions against authoritative sources and your local guidelines.

6. Third-Party Services

Our apps and website may interact with the following third parties:

  • Apple Inc. — for App Store distribution, in-app purchases, push notifications, and platform analytics.
  • Anthropic, PBC — for AI inference in apps that include Claude-powered features.
  • Google Fonts — our website serves the Sora and Source Code Pro typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

7. Data Storage & Security

Most app data is stored locally on your device. Where data leaves your device (e.g., AI prompts to Anthropic), it is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS-encrypted connections. We implement reasonable safeguards but no transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. KidLearn and similar education apps are designed for use under parental supervision and do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Your Rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and applicable international laws (such as the EU GDPR), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your local data-protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email admin@luminadevapps.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent version. Material changes will be communicated through our website or in-app notices where appropriate.
