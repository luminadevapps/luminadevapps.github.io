Delete your Angadi account

Angadi: Tamil Businesses (அங்காடி), an app by Lumina Dev Apps (Direct Parcel Distributors Inc.)

You can delete your Angadi account and the data linked to it at any time. This page explains how, and what is deleted.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Angadi on your phone.
  2. Tap the My Ads tab.
  3. Tap the account icon at the top right.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. This cannot be undone.

Ask us to delete it for you

If you cannot open the app, email support@luminadevapps.com from the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Angadi account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your Angadi account and sign-in record (name, email address and user ID).
  • Every business listing you posted, including its name, description, address, phone number and other contact details.
  • All photos you uploaded with your listings.
  • Your settings, such as blocked advertisers and hidden listings.

What is kept

  • Reports you sent about other people's listings are kept so our moderators can act on them. After your account is deleted they are linked only to an account ID that no longer identifies you.
  • Copies in routine backups held by our hosting provider (Google Firebase) are removed on the provider's normal backup schedule.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete any single listing and its photos from My Ads in the app, and keep your account.
